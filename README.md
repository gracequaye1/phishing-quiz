#  Spot the Phish

**A phishing awareness quiz by The Duchess of Hackers (Duchess Tech Space)**

Think you can tell a real message from a scam? **Spot the Phish** puts you in the seat of a cyber detective. You get 10 messages, and for each one you decide: **phishing or safe?** Then the game tells you why, so you learn the warning signs as you play.

👉 **Play it live:** https://gracequaye1.github.io/phishing-quiz/



##  Why I built this

Scam messages are everywhere, from fake Mobile Money alerts to "you have won" prizes and fake delivery texts. Many people lose money simply because nobody ever showed them what these messages look like.

I run tech education communities and I'm growing into cybersecurity, so I wanted a fun, free tool that teaches awareness without needing any technical background. The scenarios are inspired by scams people in Ghana and across West Africa really face.



##  Features

- **10 realistic scenarios** across SMS, email, WhatsApp and fake websites
- **Instant feedback** after every answer, with a clear explanation of the warning signs
- **Live score and progress bar**, plus progress dots for each question
- **Results screen** with a score-based message and confetti 
- **Local leaderboard** that saves the top 10 scores in your own browser
- **Share my score** button (uses your phone's share menu, or copies the text)
- **"Learn the Warning Signs" section** with the most common red flags
- **Mobile-friendly design** that works on phones, tablets and laptops
- **Accessibility touches:** keyboard-friendly, visible focus outlines, screen-reader updates for feedback, and reduced-motion support for people who prefer less animation

---

## Built with

- **HTML5** for structure
- **CSS3** for styling (custom properties, grid, flexbox, animations, responsive design)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** for the quiz logic, leaderboard (localStorage), sharing and confetti (canvas)

No frameworks, no libraries and no build tools. It runs by simply opening `index.html`.

##  Safety and ethics

This project is for **education and awareness only**.

- Every sender address in the quiz ends in `.example`, a reserved ending that cannot belong to a real person or company.
- No real company logos, phone numbers or links are used.
- The goal is to help people protect themselves, never to help anyone carry out scams.

**The golden rule: Stop. Check. Verify.** Never share your PIN, password or OTP, and contact organisations through their official channels.
