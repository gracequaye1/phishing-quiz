KNOWLEDGE IS PROTECTION

Learn the Warning Signs.

Before you click, pay attention. Here are some common signs of phishing.

🚨

Urgent Messages

Messages threatening to block your account or demanding immediate action.

🎁

Unexpected Prizes

You've won money or gifts in competitions you never entered.

🔐

Requests for PINs

Someone asks for your Mobile Money PIN, password or OTP.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Unexpected links that lead to unfamiliar websites or login pages.

📎

Unexpected Attachments

Unknown senders asking you to download or open strange files.

💸

Payment Requests

Requests for processing fees, delivery charges or money to claim unexpected rewards.

💡

Remember the golden rule!

Stop. Check. Verify. Never share your PIN, password or OTP. Contact the organisation through a trusted official channel if something feels suspicious.
